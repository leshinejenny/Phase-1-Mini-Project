Naga Style Kangshoi Soup

A healthy, delicious and traditional Northeast Indian-inspired vegetable soup

About the dish

Naga-style Kangshoi is a simple, healthy, and delicious vegetable soup inspired by traditional Northeast Indian cuisine. It is prepared by boiling fresh vegetables such as cabbage, potatoes, carrots, beans, and leafy greens with ginger, garlic, green chillies, dried fish and salt. Fermented bamboo shoots can be added for a unique traditional flavour. The soup is light, nutritious, and usually served hot with steamed rice. One of the special things about this dish is that it requires very little oil or no oil at all. The vegetables are boiled in water, which help preserve their natural flavours. It is a light and wholesome dish that provides vitamins, minerals, and fibres.

Naga-style Kangshoi is especially enjoyable during cold or rainy weather because it is warm and comforting. It is usually served hot with steamed rice and can be enjoyed as part of lunch or dinner. This dish reflects the simplicity of Northeast Indian cooking, where fresh ingredients, traditional flavours, and healthy preparation methods play an important role. It is easy to prepared, affordable, and a wonderful choice for everyone who enjoys homemade vegetables soups.

Recipe Information

Preparation Time: 10minutes

Cooking Time: 20minutes

Total Time: 30minutes

Serving Time: 3-4people

Cuisine: Northeast Indian Inspired

Difficulty Level: Easy

Ingredients

  • Chopped cabbage
  • 2 medium potatoes, chopped
  • 1 carrot, chopped
  • 1/2 cup green beans
  • Spinach or mustard leaves
  • Ginger, crushed
  • Garlic,crushed
  • 3-4green chillies, sliced
  • 3-4 cup of water
  • Salt according to taste
  • Fermented bamboo shoots (optional)

    • Steps to prepared Kangshoi Soup

    1. Step1: wash the vegetables

      Wash all the vegetables properly with clean water to remove dirt. Chop the cabbage, potatoes, carrots, beans, and leafy greens into small pieces.

    2. Step2: Boil the Water

      Take a large cooking pot and pour 3-4 cups of water into it. Place the pot on the stove and bring the water to a boil.

    3. Step 3: Add the vegetables

      Add chopped vegetables, potatoes, carrots, beans into the boiling water. Cook for about 10 minutes.

    4. Step 4: Add the Spices

      Add the crushed ginger, garlic, green chillies, and salt. Stir gently to mix the flavours.

    5. Step 5: Cook the soup

      Allow the vegetables to simmer untill they become soft and tender. Add more water if necessary.

    6. Step 6: Add Leafy Greens

      Add spinach or mustard leaves and cook for another 3-5 minutes.

    7. Step 7: Add Traditional

      Add a small amount of fermented bamboo shoots if desired. This gives the soup a distinctive Northeast Indian flavour.

    8. Step 8: Serve Hot

      Turn off the stove once all the vegetables are fully cooked. Transfer the soup into a bowl and serve hot with steamed rice.

    Health Benefits

    • Rich in vitamins and minerals from fresh vegetables. Contains dietary fibre that supports healthy digestion. Can be prepared without oil. Provides warmth and hydration. A nutritious way to include more vegetables in everyday meals.

Conclusion

Naga-style Kangshoi is an easy-to-make, healthy and delicious vegetable soup. Its fresh ingredients, simple preparation and comforting flavour make it a wonderful homemade dish inspired by Northeast Indian traditions.
